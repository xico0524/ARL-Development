# ARL-Development
An Adversarial Regularized Latent-factorization-of-tensors (ARL) model for missing Automatic High-Frequency Monitoring (AHFM) water quality data prediction.

The relevant code for our proposed ARL model and partial experimental samples can be found in the directory ARL-Dev, and the full AHFM datasets can be downloaded freely at https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32527353.

AHFM water quality datasets were collected from 16 June 2021 to 31 December 2025, with a sampling interval of four hours. The four columns in each dataset are separated by colons. They represent the water quality variable ID, monitoring site ID, time point ID, and the values of the water quality variables. In addition, the selected water quality indicators include water temperature (℃), pH, dissolved oxygen (mg/L), electrical conductivity (μS/cm), turbidity (NTU), chemical oxygen demand (mg/L), ammonia (mg/L), total phosphorus (mg/L), total nitrogen(mg/L), Chlorophyll-a concentration (mg/L), and algal density (cells/L). Note that the raw data were sourced from the China National Environmental Monitoring Center (National Surface Water Quality Automatic Monitoring Data Release System; https://szzdjc.cnemc.cn:8070/GJZ/Business/Publish/Main.html).
